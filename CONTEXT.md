# Spell Automation

The language used to define the spell-first automation milestone for this module.

## Language

**Baseline spell catalog**:
The spells shipped with PF2e 8.5.0, including cantrips and focus spells.

**Complete spell coverage**:
Every in-scope spell or ritual has an accounted-for workflow, with mechanical outcomes automated, player or GM choices requested where needed, and explicit exceptions for narrative adjudication and delegated afflictions. Other unsupported mechanical outcomes remain gaps unless the user explicitly accepts an exception.
_Avoid_: Fully hands-off automation

**Saving-throw ritual**:
A ritual whose own effects require a saving throw, directly or through referenced effects or condition recovery, including failure outcomes. Saves arising only from independently used created creatures or separately granted abilities do not qualify it; ritual skill checks are distinct. Optional GM-invented or substituted saves and general environmental consequences alone do not qualify a ritual; explicitly invoked save mechanics do.

**Recognized custom spell**:
A custom spell expressed through the same supported structures as official spells.

**General rules-based automation**:
Resolution derived from a spell's mechanics using rules shared across spells. Equivalent recognized mechanics receive equivalent behavior regardless of the spell's name or source identity.

**Rule primitive**:
A reusable mechanical operation or constraint, such as applying a condition, scheduling a recovery save, or limiting recovery while a stated requirement holds. Multiple primitives can express one spell's behavior.

**Casting variant**:
A distinct way to cast a spell, such as a choice of action count or form, that changes its mechanical resolution. A spell can contain both supported and unsupported casting variants.

**Cast**:
One use of a spell by a particular caster at a chosen rank and casting variant. Its consequences can include both immediate outcomes and ongoing effects.

**Resolution**:
Determining and applying a cast's mechanical outcomes from its choices, targets, and any required rolls.

**Group damage total**:
The sum of confirmed damage applied to the recipients of a displayed spell resolution, after each recipient's save outcome and damage adjustments. It excludes healing and predicted future damage.

**Spell-granted ability**:
An attack or action made available by an active spell effect. For transformations, its mechanical resolution and usage limits belong to the granting spell's coverage; unrelated creature abilities do not.

**Confirmed ending**:
An established end of a spell effect under its rules or an explicit GM correction. Uncertain tracking, disconnection, or missing tokens alone do not establish an ending.

**Detached creation**:
A spell-created summon or object that the GM has explicitly removed from automatic cleanup and now manages separately.
