# Phase 2 next-candidate cautions

2026-09-24. Corpus scan updated after the cross-family Speed modifier batch. These are assigned Phase 2 candidates; the separate 51 unassigned review spells remain deferred. Candidate names are examples of general grammar, not dispatch keys.

- **Deafness:** bounded save-wide temporary-immunity grammar now enables this one spell; Blindness remains unchanged. Fear the Sun has the same `is then temporarily immune` preamble but remains gated by conditional Light Blindness/Blinded behavior. Touch of Obedience is now enabled by shared pronoun immunity and affirmative unlinked Prone grammar, with independent Stupefied/Prone lifetimes.
- **Unraveling Blast:** now enabled by typed-half damage with a unique matching structured component and condition-clause timing. Stunned 1 remains action debt; Off-Guard expires at the appropriate upcoming target or caster turn. The live batch also corrected the native upcoming-turn offset.
- **Pain of Ages:** enabled. The causal suffix is a guarded flavor clause and its Stunned is timed Stunned.
- **Vomit Swarm:** failure Sickened 1 uses `A creature that fails its saving throw also becomes...`; the final sentence says `Once the spell ends, the swarm disappears`. That lifecycle sentence must be accounted for before treating the rider as complete.
- **Haunting Hymn:** critical-failure Deafened is otherwise simple, but the opening `only creatures in the area can hear` is a hearing eligibility rule; a rider parser alone must not erase it.
- **Fungal Exhalation:** failure Sickened 1 and critical-failure Sickened 2 are followed by `A creature that doesn't breathe is immune to this effect`. Enabling requires both distinct degree mapping and a live nonbreathing immunity check.
- **Weird:** `any number of creatures` is the sole pending capability, but the critical-failure text contains a secondary Fortitude save that can kill or apply double damage/Fleeing. Its compiled critical-failure outcome is empty despite residual prose being marked complete; target grammar alone would be unsafe.

- **Ancient Dust, Cutting Insult and Bursting Bloom:** now enabled by exact two-component selection grammar, validated against structured damage and heightening. Success excludes persistent damage. Critical failure doubles persistent damage for the first two, but leaves Bursting Bloom’s persistent damage unchanged. Native zero-roll/minimum semantics are preserved. Other mixed damage spells remain gated by their individual unresolved mechanics.

- **Tempest Touch:** now composes native damage and a degree-selected native Speed Effect. The shared compiler proves matching save wording, ChoiceSet/FlatModifier mechanics and duration. Broader linked native Effects still need complete semantics; a UUID link alone is not sufficient. See [family audit](../../reports/phase-spell-batches/composition-family-audit.md).

- **Existing-route corrections:** Eagle’s Cry and Painful Vibrations no longer borrow the following condition’s timer. Gravity Wave, Blazing Fissure and Stormburst now apply the previously omitted unlinked critical-failure Prone. See [clause-family evidence](../verification/phase-2-clause-family.md).

- **Grim Tendrils and Ulcerous Canker:** enabled by a shared proof of structured initial damage, one inline rank-dependent bleed expression, degree multipliers and matching heightening. The runtime composes a single native application before any HP mutation. Live verification passed all degrees, legal ranks, IWR and preflight; see [batch evidence](../verification/phase-2-3-inline-family.md).

- **Dehydrate:** remains blocked by its recurring save and hydration choice. **Gritty Wheeze** is enabled through the compiled water/plant degree adjustment, not by discarding the clause.

- **Chilling Spray and Magical Fetters:** enabled by generic generated Speed modifiers, independent lifetimes and proven outcome inheritance. The whole related family audit covers 254 unique spells; recognition of one modifier never enables a spell with outstanding mechanics. See [cross-family verification](../verification/phase-2-3-speed-family.md).

- **Grasping Grave and Shadow's Web:** enabled by the timer-or-Escape event lifetime and the positional condition-list grammar. Escape is graded against the snapshotted spell DC, and the GM confirms which restraint ended. The list grammar also exposed conditions previously dropped from 15 blocked spells; they remain blocked by their own gaps. See the [event-lifetime verification](../verification/phase-2-3-escape-family.md).
- **Vampiric Maiden:** critical failure now compiles fully; the failure branch ends on “an Interact action to extricate itself”, an unsupported event. Do not map it to Escape.
- **Synaptic Pulse, Pain of Ages and Dizzying Colors:** enabled with timed Stunned, a named timed Effect with no action-debt recovery. **Wails of the Damned:** blocked only by a rolled condition value (`Drained [[/r 1d4]]`); per-target versus per-cast rolling must be decided from the rules before implementing.
- **Next foundational candidates:** basic-save condition riders beyond the fixed prefix list (Radiant Beam, Spout, Remember the Lost, Rip the Spirit) share one grammar gap but each carries a second rule (darkness counteract, area change, optional chant, action variants). Creature-state immunities (doesn't breathe, lacks organs, can't hear) need GM-confirmed eligibility. Conditional area membership, recovery-linked modifiers and recurring triggers remain separate capabilities.

- **Confusion and Paralyze:** enabled by recurring end-of-turn saves (`end` and `reduce` modes); the official Paralyze slug still uses its dedicated adapter. See [recurring-save verification](../verification/phase-2-3-recurring-family.md).
- **Degree-shift preambles:** Gritty Wheeze (trait) and Spiritual Anamnesis (GM-confirmed) now compile. Heat Metal, Hypnopompic Terrors, Eject Soul, Dehydrate and the others still carry `preamble:degree-adjustment` because their wording differs: Heat Metal's improvement comes from choosing to Release the item, Hypnopompic Terrors shifts only sleeping targets and also wakes them, and Eject Soul lets allies choose their degree. Add each wording to the grammar only with its exact map; never enable them by ignoring the preamble. Breath of Drought's adjustment compiles, but it stays blocked by its area-entry trigger.
- **Confusing Cry:** blocked by critical-success temporary immunity and a self-Strike that suppresses Confused's flat check. **Waking Dream:** Sustain.
- **Damage-derived family:** Vision of Death, Vampiric Feast, Vampiric Exsanguination, Devour Life and All-Encompassing Hunger are enabled ([verification](../verification/phase-2-3-damage-derived-family.md)). Drain Life (blood-magic reference and HP-lost cap), Soul Siphon (HP lost to Drained), Life-Draining Roots (per-creature 1d4) and Vampiric Revelry (action variants) each need their own rule. Never widen the benefit grammar by dropping those clauses.
- **Self-help batch:** Dazzling Flash, Slime Spit, Grasp of the Deep, Boil Blood and Reblooded are enabled; **Pollen Pods was withdrawn**, because its four motion-triggered bulbs need trigger/placement management ([verification](../verification/phase-2-3-self-help-family.md)). Fungal Exhalation's “immune to this effect” is scope-ambiguous and must stay blocked. Vampiric Maiden now only lacks its caster benefit and its trapped flavor clause.
- **Damage-gated stings:** enabled through `resolution.damageGate`. The save is still rolled before Finalize, but its result is ignored for a target that takes no delivery damage.
- **Affliction stage effects** are never applied by this module; Pseudo Afflictions owns them. Its registry lacks Blood Chestnuts, Blinding Bottle, Advanced Scurvy and Blister Bomb, so the card asks the GM to apply those manually. Pseudo Afflictions applies all three Divine Plagues conditions instead of the caster's single choice; that's its modelling, not this module's.
- **Affliction-manipulating spells** are enabled through the affliction-care route. It changes Pseudo Afflictions' Effect badge using PF2e's affliction rules, the same thing its badge controls do, and never calls its private save routine. If Pseudo Afflictions changes its data model (badge = stage, `flags.pseudo-afflictions.onset/stages/save`), revisit `scripts/affliction-care-runtime.js`.
- **Implied condition value 1** applies only to a bare link granted by “becomes/is” and followed by a plain continuation. Wails of the Damned's “{Drained} [[/r 1d4]]” must stay a rolled-value gap.
- **Condition-floor family:** only the exact “becomes X N and can't reduce its X condition below M while the curse remains” form compiles. Buzzing Bites, Ghoulish Cravings, Infectious Ennui, Unsettling Knowledge, Unspeakable Shadow and Lift Nature's Caul use other floor wordings and lifetimes, and stay blocked.
