- **Cloak of Shadow:** The light it dims and the concealment that follows are the GM's lighting call.
- **Distortion Lens:** Range through the lens depends on each attack's path.
- **Illuminate:** Which light sources are non-magical fire is the GM's call.
- **Swarmsense:** A scouting swarm with its own AC and saves; any damage ends it.
- **Vanishing Tracks:** It raises other creatures' Track DC, which the GM sets.
- **Vector Screen:** The penalty applies to attacks whose path crosses the screen.
- **Verdant Sprout:** The plant becomes cover and difficult terrain only after 10 minutes, where it lands.
