# Cantrips and rank 1: the 16 spells left for your decision

2026-09-27, after batch 119 (census **600 enabled**).

Every other cantrip and rank-1 spell now resolves, either automatically or as a counted note-only spell. Each of these 16 needs a judgment call about how much to approximate, which I didn't want to make for you. For each one:
- **What it needs:** what the text asks for.
- **Why it's blocked:** what the module can't do yet.
- **Options:** (A) is the recommended build, unless I say otherwise; (B) is a note-only fallback. A note-only fallback is counted apart, like the other 369 note-only spells.
- **Recommendation.**

## Sustained spells whose Sustain makes the spell's attack

### Dancing Blade (cantrip)
- **What it needs:**
  - On cast, the weapon Strikes a target using your spell attack modifier, for 3d6 damage of the weapon's type (you pick for a versatile weapon).
  - Each Sustain: Strike again, or change partners.
  - The attacks count toward your multiple attack penalty.
- **Why it's blocked:** there's no route for "each Sustain makes the attack". PF2e's item stores the damage as bludgeoning.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** A spell-attack card on cast, then an "Attack again (Sustain)" control while sustained. The damage type is the caster's pick (bludgeoning, piercing or slashing). The multiple attack penalty and "change partners" are notes; the GM retargets.
  - **(B)** Note only.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

### Spirit Object (cantrip)
- **What it needs:**
  - The object Strides 25 feet.
  - Each Sustain: move it again, or have it Strike with a melee spell attack for 2d4 bludgeoning, piercing or slashing (as fits the object).
  - Recasting reverts the previous object.
- **Why it's blocked:** the same missing route as Dancing Blade, and the target is an object.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** The same "attack on Sustain" route, with the damage type as the caster's pick. Movement and the recast are GM notes.
  - **(B)** Note only.
- **Recommendation:** (A), built together with Dancing Blade.

## Effects PF2e doesn't provide

### Cloak of Shadow (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - Dim light in a 20-foot emanation that follows the target, counting as magical darkness.
  - The target can use its concealment to Hide.
  - The cloak can be dropped as a decoy.
- **Why it's blocked:** it's lighting. The module doesn't manage light, and PF2e has no Effect.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** Give the target's token a darkness light source for the duration. This edits the token's light settings.
  - **(B)** A note Effect.
- **Recommendation:** (B). Changing token lighting is invasive.

### Invoke True Name (cantrip)
- **What it needs:** until the end of the turn, the target:
  - is Off-Guard against your spells;
  - takes −2 circumstance to saves against your spells;
  - the first time your spells damage it, has weakness equal to double the rank to that damage.
- **Why it's blocked:** PF2e has no Effect, and "against your spells" needs origin predicates.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** A generated Effect with predicates on the spell's origin, and the weakness removed after the first damage (a hook). This needs PF2e's roll options checked live.
  - **(B)** A note Effect.
- **Recommendation:** (A) if you want it exact; it's the riskiest build here.

### Shielded Arm (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - Raise a Shield with the arm (+2 AC).
  - Shield Block with Hardness 4 and 15 HP (+4 and +15 per 2 ranks), no Broken Threshold.
  - Ends when the HP are gone; anathema text.
- **Why it's blocked:** it's a shield, and PF2e models shields as items.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** A temporary PF2e shield item on the target, which PF2e's own Raise a Shield and Shield Block use; it's removed when the spell ends or its HP reach 0. This adds an item to the inventory.
  - **(B)** An Effect with a +2 AC toggle, plus a note for blocking.
- **Recommendation:** (A) if adding a temporary item is acceptable to you; otherwise (B).

### Wish Market (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - A +1 status bonus to Perception and a chosen skill (+2 at 4th rank, +3 at 7th).
  - A free Recall Knowledge or Seek.
  - A random price from a 1d4 table (−2 to two skills).
  - Each Sustain: a free Recall Knowledge or Seek, or haggle to suppress the price for 1 round.
- **Why it's blocked:** there's no Effect, and it needs a roll table plus a skill choice.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** A generated Effect with the chosen skill bonus, and a rolled price penalty (the card rolls 1d4). A Sustain control suppresses the penalty for a round; the free actions are notes.
  - **(B)** A note Effect.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

### Hand of the Apprentice (1)
- **What it needs:** a spell attack roll, then the weapon's damage as a melee Strike, with Int instead of Str. Critical: double damage and critical specialization. PF2e's Effect handles the Int and specialization.
- **Why it's blocked:** a spell attack followed by the weapon's own Strike damage roll doesn't fit either the attack route or the Strike route.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** The card applies PF2e's Effect and rolls the spell attack; on a hit it prompts you to roll the chosen weapon's damage. That's guided, not fully automatic.
  - **(B)** Note only.
- **Recommendation:** (A), guided.

## Stateful spells (hit points, mirrors, adhesion)

### Shattering Gem (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - A gem with 5 HP (+5 a rank). Each Strike against the target makes it roll a DC 11 flat check; on a success the gem absorbs the damage first.
  - At 0 HP, the gem deals 1d8 slashing (basic Reflex) to the creature that broke it, if within 10 feet.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** An Effect with a gem-HP badge. A "Struck: roll the flat check" control lowers the badge, and at 0 opens the shatter save.
  - **(B)** A note Effect.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

### Trickster's Mirrors (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - 1 to 3 mirrors (one more each Sustain). An attack that would hit you has a mirror chance by flat roll.
  - A failed but not critically failed attack destroys a mirror; an adjacent attacker takes 1d4 mental (basic Will).
  - Area damage breaks every mirror.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** An Effect with a mirror-count badge; controls for "attack would hit: roll the mirror check" and "mirror broken: the adjacent attacker's Will save".
  - **(B)** A note Effect.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

### Stick On (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - You and the target stick together until it Escapes.
  - By size: a smaller target is Immobilized and pulled along; the same size is difficult terrain for both; a larger target leaves *you* Immobilized.
  - You can Dismiss it.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** Conditions picked by comparing sizes on the card (Immobilized with an Escape end; difficult terrain as a note), plus Dismiss.
  - **(B)** A note Effect.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

### Grasping Vine (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - An object target moves 10 feet (on cast and on Sustain).
  - A creature target saves: success means Grabbed until the end of your next turn unless it Escapes (your DC) or destroys the vine (AC 15, 10 HP); failure means Grabbed until it Escapes or destroys the vine; critical failure adds an immediate 10-foot Reposition. Each Sustain can Reposition it 5 feet. The spell ends when it escapes.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** A degree save with Grabbed and the Escape lifetime (existing). The spell ends on escape. The vine's AC/HP, the Repositions and the object branch are GM notes.
  - **(B)** Note only.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

## Larger rule sets

### Dehydrate (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - 1d6 persistent fire with a basic Fortitude save; water and plant creatures get one degree worse.
  - The spell ends for a creature when its persistent damage ends.
  - Each of its turns, an extra Fortitude save: failure is Enfeebled 1 and critical failure Enfeebled 2, until the end of its next turn. It can skip the save if it drank within the last round.
- **Why it's blocked:** it needs a per-creature recurring save that lasts as long as its persistent damage.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** Build it: the basic save and degree adjustment (existing), then a per-creature Ongoing record that opens the extra save at each of its turn ends while its persistent fire lasts, with a GM "drank?" question.
  - **(B)** Automate the first save; the extra save becomes a note.
- **Recommendation:** (A). It's the largest build left.

### Grease (1)
- **What it needs:**
  - The caster chooses an area or an object.
  - Area: creatures on the grease make a Reflex save **or an Acrobatics check** or fall Prone; creatures moving onto it must Balance.
  - Object: its holder rolls Acrobatics or Reflex, taking −2 to using it on a failure and dropping it on a critical failure; a worn object gives +2 against grapples.
- **Decision needed:** the module rolls saves, not "save or skill check".
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** Area branch: roll the Reflex save and note that the creature may use Acrobatics instead. Movement is a Balance reminder. Object branch: notes.
  - **(A′)** As (A), but also let the GM enter an Acrobatics result instead.
  - **(B)** Note only.
- **Recommendation:** (A).

### Manifest Will (cantrip)
- **What it needs:**
  - A sustained emanation whose effect depends on your patron's tradition.
  - **Arcane:** weakness to spell damage.
  - **Divine:** temporary Hit Points.
  - **Occult:** lesser cover for allies.
  - **Primal:** a −10 Speed penalty until Escaped.
  - You're concealed to creatures outside it. PF2e has an Aura and an Effect with tradition-picked rules.
- **Why it's blocked:** four branches, PF2e's aura, and Escape and cover interactions.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** Use PF2e's own Aura, proving each tradition's rule against the text; the Escape end is a control. It's a substantial proof job.
  - **(B)** Apply PF2e's Aura as-is, plus a note (the Effect + note route).
- **Recommendation:** (B) now, (A) later if you want it exact.

### Delay Consequence (1)
- **What it needs:** as a reaction, delay an attack's damage and effects until the spell ends (1 round), then apply them. You can Dismiss it.
- **Why it's blocked:** PF2e applies an attack's damage and effects itself; the module can't hold and replay them safely.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** A control that records the attack's damage message and re-applies it when the spell ends. Only the damage; other effects stay the GM's.
  - **(B)** Note only.
- **Recommendation:** (B). (A) risks double-applying damage.

### Pyroclastic Truth (1, impossible spell)
- **What it needs:**
  - A huge 2d12 fire burst.
  - Creatures it damages are marked; later Fuel actions deal persistent fire to the marked.
  - The caster carries a lethologic mark: Frightened on fire damage, and instant death at 0 HP from fire.
- **Why it's blocked:** the unique Fuel mechanic and the story-level caster mark.
- **Options:**
  - **(A)** The basic save, plus a "marked" Effect on damaged creatures and a Fuel control. The caster's mark is a note.
  - **(B)** Leave it.
- **Recommendation:** (B). It's rare and campaign-specific.
